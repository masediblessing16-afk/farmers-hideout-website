WELCOME TO YOUR HIDEOUT

Good food feels better in nature.

Welcome to The Farmer's Hideout, a cozy local food stand in Mogapeng Village. Surrounded by nature, we're a relaxed place to catch up with friends, take a break and enjoy something delicious.

From saucy chicken wings and loaded kotas to burgers, platters and refreshing drinks, there's something for every craving.
